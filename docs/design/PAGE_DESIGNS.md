# Storefront design

Nivaro uses a blue-led visual system with dark and light themes, multicolor hardware lighting, and Persian RTL and English LTR layouts. Local Vazirmatn and Inter fonts cover both languages. Use tabular numbers for prices and respect reduced-motion preferences.

| Surface | Design and interaction |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Place the headline opposite the hardware artwork. Keep the case in frame at narrow and wide widths and expose catalog and builder entry points. [English storefront](../evidence/storefront-en-dark.png) · [Persian storefront](../evidence/storefront-fa-dark.png) |
| Catalog | Offer named filters and a separate market selector. Keep filtering, results, and empty states readable on mobile. |
| Product detail | Show the exact model and package, sourced specifications, an independently priced market offer, and its observation date. Identify illustrative artwork on the product page. [Product detail](../evidence/product-detail-en-dark.png) |
| PC builder | Group core parts and optional peripherals, show the total for the selected quantities, and allow one or more selections to be added to the cart. [PC builder](../evidence/pc-builder-en-dark.png) |
| Comparison | Align comparable facts and show missing values explicitly. Keep the system comparison separate from the parts picker. |
| Cart and checkout | Preserve named groups and individual selections. Show availability and final totals at checkout. [Checkout](../evidence/simulator-checkout-en-mobile.png) |
| Customer and staff areas | Keep navigation and permissions scoped to the signed-in role. Use separate sign-in surfaces and show only the actions allowed by the current state. [Customer support](../evidence/support-fa-mobile.png) |
